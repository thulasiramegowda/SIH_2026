# 🛰️ MANGANAI
### Satellite Intelligence for Manganese Prospectivity

**AI-Powered Mineral Exploration using Satellite Imagery and Machine Learning**

MANGANAI is an AI and satellite-based decision-support system designed to identify and prioritize potential manganese exploration zones using Earth observation data, machine learning, and geospatial analysis.

The project explores how satellite-derived indicators and AI/ML techniques can support mineral prospectivity analysis by helping identify areas that may deserve further geological investigation.

Currently being developed as a **University Hackathon MVP**, MANGANAI aims to bring together remote sensing, machine learning, and geospatial visualization into a unified exploration-support platform.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional mineral exploration can involve extensive field investigations, geological surveys, and data analysis.

Identifying promising exploration zones requires analyzing different sources of geological and environmental information.

MANGANAI explores the use of satellite-derived information and machine learning to support this process by helping prioritize potential exploration areas for further investigation.

## 💡 Project Objective

To develop an AI-powered decision-support system that combines:

- **Satellite Imagery:** Explore Earth observation data for relevant surface indicators.
- **Remote Sensing:** Analyze satellite-derived information for potential mineral prospectivity.
- **Machine Learning:** Explore patterns and relationships in the available data.
- **Geospatial Visualization:** Present potential exploration zones in an understandable geographic format.
- **Decision Support:** Help prioritize areas for additional geological investigation.

## ⚠️ Important Note

MANGANAI is an experimental decision-support project.

Its potential exploration zones should not be interpreted as confirmed manganese deposits or substitutes for geological surveys, field validation, or professional mineral exploration assessments.

## 🎓 Project Background

Developed as part of a university hackathon initiative, MANGANAI explores the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Earth Observation, Remote Sensing, and Geospatial Technology.

The goal is to experiment with how emerging technologies can contribute to more informed and data-driven mineral exploration.
